Mini Emergency Exit Planner

A fire alarm sounds. The planner must get the person to an exit fast without walking into smoke. The fastest route runs through a corridor with a noisy smoke sensor. Three AI methods solve this together:

  1. A*fastest route + detour
  2. BayesP(corridor blocked)
  3. MDPProceed or Detour?
  4. Plansafe route out

Model assumptions (toy values, no real data)
  • Map: 6×6 grid, 2 exits, 3 walls, 1 smoke sensor in the corridor doorway. Every move costs 1 step.
  • Sensor: P(alarm | blocked) = 0.90, P(alarm | clear) = 0.15; prior P(blocked) = 0.20.
  • Rewards: Proceed + Clear = −(fastest route); Proceed + Blocked = −(walk to the corridor and back + detour) − smoke penalty; Detour = −(detour route).
  • Transitions T(s′ | s, a): Proceed keeps a blocked corridor blocked (1.0) and a clear one clear with 0.90; Detour leads to a clear route with 0.95.
